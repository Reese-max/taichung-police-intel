# Product

## Purpose

Reduce one to two hours of daily public-information monitoring to a five-minute review of material Taichung police policy and council issues. Every conclusion must return to official evidence.

## Primary users

- Staff preparing council briefings and follow-up research.
- Analysts monitoring official policy, budget, procurement, and legislative changes.

## Core journey

1. Review at most ten items in the five-minute homepage.
2. Open tonight's or tomorrow's council preparation.
3. Inspect historical questions, oral answers, proposals, and policy milestones.
4. Open the evidence drawer and return to an official URL, PDF page, or video timestamp.

## Non-goals

- No internal police data, 110 calls, criminal case data, personal data, dispatch, or emergency command.
- No general-purpose chatbot.
- No claim that a resolution or oral answer proves post-meeting implementation.
- No broad source expansion before the end-to-end core journey works.

## Success rule

A feature is complete only when its executable acceptance command passes. Code presence, a running process, an AI statement, or a screenshot alone is not completion evidence.
