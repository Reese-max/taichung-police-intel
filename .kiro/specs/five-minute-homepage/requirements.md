# Five-minute homepage requirements

## Goal

Help a reviewer understand today's material police-policy changes and their evidence in five minutes or less.

## Requirements

### R1 — Bounded list

WHEN the homepage loads THE SYSTEM SHALL show no more than ten `AUTO_PASS` and `HOME_CANDIDATE` items.

### R2 — Explainable priority

WHEN an item is displayed THE SYSTEM SHALL show a deterministic reason code, what changed, the next known milestone, evidence count, source health, and window completeness.

### R3 — Evidence access

WHEN a reviewer opens an item THE SYSTEM SHALL provide a direct path to its official URL, PDF locator, or official video timestamp.

### R4 — Central-policy limit

WHEN central-policy items qualify THE SYSTEM SHALL show no more than two and shall separate the official stage from any inferred local impact.

### R5 — Safe degradation

WHEN summary generation or verification is unavailable THE SYSTEM SHALL display verified source text and deterministic reason codes rather than unverified generated text.

### R6 — English path

WHEN a judge selects the English path THE SYSTEM SHALL expose the complete primary journey and limitations in English.
